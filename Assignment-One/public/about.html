About Us

Nishiura Baseball Club was formed in 1987 by principal Yasujiro Ozu. He, alongside coach Kenji Mizoguchi, selected the very first team in our school's history. Under the captainship of Akira Kurosawa, Nishiura placed 3rd in the regional championships of 1988.

Today, our team consists of star pitcher Ren Mihashi, team captain Azusa Hanai and cleanup hitter Yuuichirou Tajima, just to name a few of our players.

We're excited to see the next generation of talent join our illustrious team at the start of this semester.
